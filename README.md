# Retail Sales Analysis 2023

A data analytics project using retail transaction data from Kaggle to identify customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and the drivers behind the Q3 sales decline.
 
## Project Overview
 
This project analyzes retail sales data obtained from Kaggle to identify customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and sales trends throughout 2023.

The analysis focuses on understanding the Q3 sales decline and identifying opportunities to improve revenue consistency throughout the year.

---

## Business Problem

Although the retailer generated ₱456,000 in annual sales, revenue declined significantly during Q3, making it the weakest-performing quarter.

### Business Question

How can the retailer reduce the Q3 sales decline and maintain more consistent revenue throughout the year?

---

## Dataset Information

- Source: Kaggle Retail Sales Dataset
- Transactions: 1,000
- Total Sales: ₱456,000
- Units Sold: 2,514

## Key Findings

### Q3 Recorded the Lowest Sales Performance
Q3 generated the lowest sales among all quarters, indicating a seasonal slowdown.

### Electronics Is the Strongest Revenue Driver and a Key Lever for Growth
Electronics generated the highest sales revenue and remained the retailer's strongest product category.

### Customers Aged 25-54 Drive Most Retail Revenue
Customers aged 25–54 consistently represented the highest-spending customer groups.

### Gender Spending Differences Create Opportunities for Targeted Promotions
Gender spending differences create opportunities for more targeted promotions.

---

## Recommendations

#### 1. Increase Q3 sales through Electronics bundle promotions targeted at high-value customers aged 25-54.


#### 2. Improve promotional effectiveness through age- and gender-based customer segmentation campaigns.
